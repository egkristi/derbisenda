# DERBI SENDA R 2004 -- Complete Owner Guide

## Vehicle Information

-   Model: DERBI SENDA R 2004
-   Engine: 49cc 2-stroke
-   Power: 2.6 kW (4 HP)
-   Top Speed: 45 km/h
-   Weight: 96 kg
-   Fuel: Petrol
-   Transmission: Manual
-   Tires:
    -   Front: 80/90-21
    -   Rear: 110/80-18

## Engine

-   Type: 2-stroke single cylinder
-   Cooling: Liquid cooled
-   Carburetor: Dell'Orto PHVA 14mm (typical)
-   Spark plug: NGK B8ES
-   Oil: 2T full synthetic

## Fuel / Oil Mixing

-   With oil pump: No premix required
-   Premix:
    -   Normal: 1:50
    -   Hard use: 1:40
    -   Break-in: 1:33

## Carburetor Settings

-   Main jet: 72--75
-   Idle jet: 34--36
-   Needle: middle clip
-   Air screw: 1.5--2.5 turns out

## Carburetor Adjustment

1.  Warm engine
2.  Set idle screw
3.  Adjust air screw for highest idle
4.  Re-adjust idle

## Electrical Wiring Overview

-   Stator → CDI → Coil → Spark Plug
-   Kill switch to CDI
-   Regulator for lights

## Maintenance

### Every 500 km

-   Clean air filter
-   Check chain
-   Check brakes

### Every 2000 km

-   Clean carburetor
-   Change spark plug
-   Check coolant

### Every 5000 km

-   Check piston and rings
-   Check bearings

## Torque Specs

-   Spark plug: 18 Nm
-   Cylinder head: 12 Nm
-   Wheel axle: 60 Nm

## Troubleshooting

### No start

-   Check spark
-   Check fuel
-   Clean carb

### Poor performance

-   Check jets
-   Check air filter

### Overheating

-   Check coolant
-   Check radiator
