## Carburetor Setup

### Standard settings

-   Main jet: 70-75
-   Idle jet: 34
-   Needle: Middle position
-   Air screw: 1.5--2 turns out

### Adjustment procedure

1.  Warm engine
2.  Set idle screw
3.  Adjust air screw to highest RPM
4.  Lower idle again

### Symptoms

-   Rich: Black spark plug
-   Lean: White spark plug
-   Perfect: Brown spark plug

## Maintenance

### Every 1000 km

-   Clean carburetor
-   Check spark plug
-   Check chain tension

### Every 3000 km

-   Replace spark plug
-   Check brake pads
-   Check coolant
